# CampusTrace System Knowledge Base

## System Overview

CampusTrace is an AI-powered lost and found platform for universities. It helps students report lost items, find found items, and uses AI matching to connect lost items with found items.

---

## Main Features

### 1. Smart AI Matching

- Automatically matches lost items with found items using AI
- Uses text similarity and image similarity
- Shows match percentage (60%+ is good, 80%+ is excellent)
- Users get notifications when matches are found

### 2. Item Reporting

- Users can report LOST items or FOUND items
- Required information: title, description, category, location, date
- Optional: upload photos (helps with AI matching)
- Items go through moderation before being published

### 3. Claiming Process

- Users can claim items they think are theirs
- Provide verification details to prove ownership
- Finder reviews the claim
- If approved, both parties can arrange handover

### 4. Handover System

- Secure item exchange between finder and owner
- Both parties must confirm the handover
- System tracks handover status
- Items marked as "Recovered" after successful handover

## User Processes

### How to Report a Lost Item

1. Go to "Post Item" screen
2. Select "Lost" status
3. Fill in item details:
   - Title (e.g., "Blue Backpack")
   - Description (be specific!)
   - Category (select from dropdown)
   - Location where lost
   - Date when lost
4. Upload photos if available
5. Submit for review
6. Wait for moderation approval
7. Once approved, AI will search for matches
8. You'll get notifications if matches are found

### How to Report a Found Item

1. Go to "Post Item" screen
2. Select "Found" status
3. Fill in item details:
   - Title (e.g., "iPhone 13")
   - Description (describe the item)
   - Category
   - Location where found
   - Date when found
4. Upload photos
5. Submit for review
6. Wait for moderation approval
7. Once approved, owners can claim it

### How to Claim an Item

1. Browse items or check AI matches
2. Find an item that might be yours
3. Click "Claim This Item"
4. Provide verification details:
   - Describe unique features
   - Provide proof of ownership
   - Answer security questions
5. Submit claim
6. Wait for finder to review
7. If approved, coordinate handover

### How Handover Works

1. After claim is approved, handover process starts
2. Both parties agree on meeting location and time
3. Meet in a safe, public place (recommended: campus security office)
4. Exchange the item
5. Both parties confirm handover in the app
6. Item is marked as "Recovered"
7. Both users earn points and badges!

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: How long does moderation take?

A: Usually 24-48 hours. Admins review items to prevent spam and ensure quality.

### Q: What if no one claims my found item?

A: Keep it posted! You can also turn it in to campus security. The item stays visible until claimed or you remove it.

### Q: How accurate is AI matching?

A: Very accurate! It uses both text and image analysis. Matches above 80% are highly reliable.

### Q: Is my personal information safe?

A: Yes! We only share necessary contact info after both parties agree to handover.

### Q: Can I edit my post after submission?

A: Yes, go to "My Posts" and click edit. Changes may require re-moderation.

### Q: What if someone falsely claims my found item?

A: You review all claims and can reject suspicious ones. Ask for proof of ownership.

### Q: How do I contact the finder/owner?

A: Use the in-app messaging system. Click "Message" on the item page.

### Q: Can I delete my post?

A: Yes, go to "My Posts" and select delete. This is permanent.

### Q: What happens after handover?

A: Item is marked "Recovered", both users earn points, and the item is archived.

### Q: How do I change my notification settings?

A: Go to Settings > Notification Preferences. You can customize what alerts you receive.

### Q: Can I use the app on multiple devices?

A: Yes! Your account syncs across all devices.

### Q: What if I forgot my password?

A: Click "Forgot Password" on login screen. You'll receive a reset email.

### Q: How do I report inappropriate content?

A: Click the report button on any item or message. Admins will review it.

### Q: Can I search for items by image?

A: Not directly, but AI matching uses images to find similar items automatically.

### Q: What's the difference between Lost and Found status?

A: "Lost" means you lost something and are looking for it. "Found" means you found something and want to return it.

## Tips for Success

### For Reporting Lost Items:

- Be very specific in your description
- Include unique identifiers (scratches, stickers, serial numbers)
- Upload clear photos
- Check back regularly for matches
- Respond quickly to claims

### For Reporting Found Items:

- Don't include too many identifying details (to prevent false claims)
- Upload photos
- Review claims carefully
- Ask for proof of ownership
- Meet in safe, public places for handover

### For Claiming Items:

- Provide detailed verification
- Be honest
- Respond promptly
- Be respectful to finders
- Thank them for their help!

## Safety Guidelines

1. Always meet in public, well-lit areas
2. Bring a friend if possible
3. Campus security office is the best meeting spot
4. Don't share personal addresses
5. Use in-app messaging only
6. Report suspicious behavior
7. Trust your instincts
