What is CampusTrace?

CampusTrace is a smart, AI-powered lost and found web application designed specifically for university communities. We help students, faculty, and staff find their lost belongings through intelligent AI matching, secure messaging, and a verified campus network.

Key Features

  • AI-Powered Matching - Our intelligent system automatically matches lost items with found reports
  • Secure Messaging - Communicate safely with finders through our in-app messaging
  • University Verification - Verified campus users for a trusted community
  • Real-Time Notifications - Get instant alerts when your item is found
  • Mobile App - Available for Android devices

For Universities

CampusTrace is designed to serve university communities, providing a centralized platform for lost and found item management. Reduce administrative burden while helping your campus community stay connected.
